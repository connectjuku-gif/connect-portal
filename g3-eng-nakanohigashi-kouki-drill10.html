Yuto is a junior high school student who became interested in fair trade after a class discussion last year. He learned that farmers who grow cacao and coffee in developing countries often receive very little money for their hard work.

One weekend, Yuto found a small fair trade market that was held near his station. He talked to a seller who told him about the lives of farmers who grow the products. Yuto was moved by the stories he heard, and he decided to tell his family about fair trade.

At first, his parents were not very interested in products that cost a little more than usual. Yuto explained why fair trade is important for farmers around the world, and his parents finally agreed to try some fair trade coffee.

Now, Yuto's family buys fair trade products once a month. Yuto says, "I want to keep learning about products that can make the world a little better."

(1) Why did Yuto become interested in fair trade?
ア Because he read a book about farmers.イ Because of a discussion in his class last year.
ウ Because his parents told him to learn about it.エ Because he visited a foreign country.
(2) What did Yuto do after talking to the seller at the market?
ア He decided to become a seller himself.イ He stopped buying fair trade products.
ウ He decided to tell his family about fair trade.エ He moved to a different town.
(3) How did Yuto's parents feel about fair trade products at first?
ア They were very excited to try them.イ They were not very interested because of the price.
ウ They refused to listen to Yuto at all.エ They had already been buying them for years.
(4) 次は本文の要約です。( ① )・( ② )に入る最も適切なものを、それぞれア～エから選びなさい。
Yuto became interested in fair trade after a class discussion, and he learned that farmers who grow cacao and coffee often receive ( ① ) money for their work. After visiting a fair trade market and talking to a seller, Yuto told his family about it. His parents were not interested at first because of the ( ② ), but they finally agreed to try fair trade coffee.
① ア a lot ofイ very little
ウ noエ enough
② ア tasteイ colorウ priceエ size

解答・解説

大問10型ドリル／配点：1＝12点、2＝12点　合計24点
■ 出題範囲メモ・作問方針 実際の中野東3年定期考査 大問10（長文読解・内容一致×3＋要約選択×2、計24点）の形式を踏襲し、後期中間テストの範囲（Program5＝フェアトレード、Program6＝海洋プラスチック）のテーマで初見の書き下ろし長文を2題用意した。どちらの文章にも関係代名詞（who／that／which）を複数回使い、文法知識と読解力を同時に確認できるようにしている。

1　Yuto（フェアトレード）

(1) イ　(2) ウ　(3) イ　(4) ① イ（very little）　② ウ（price）
(1) 第1段落「became interested in fair trade after a class discussion last year」より、授業での話し合いがきっかけ。(2) 第2段落「he decided to tell his family about fair trade」より、家族に伝えようと決めた。(3) 第3段落「his parents were not very interested in products that cost a little more than usual」より、値段が理由で興味がなかった。(4) ①第1段落「farmers... often receive very little money」よりvery little。②第3段落「not very interested in products that cost a little more」＝price（値段）が理由。

2　Mio（海洋プラスチック清掃ロボット）

(1) ア　(2) イ　(3) イ　(4) ① イ（collects）　② ウ（engineer）
(1) 第1段落「showed the class a news video about a robot that can collect plastic from the sea」より、海のプラスチックを集めるロボットの動画。(2) 第2段落「She decided to join a science club... to learn more about robots that help the environment」より。(3) 第3段落「Many visitors who came to see their model asked a lot of questions about ocean plastic」より。(4) ①第1段落「a robot that can collect plastic from the sea」よりcollects。②最終文「studying hard to become an engineer herself」よりengineer。
■ この予想問題のねらい（生徒への指示） ・内容一致は、選択肢を本文の該当箇所と1文ずつ照らし合わせ、「本文に書かれていない」「本文と反対のこと」を言っている選択肢を消していく練習をする。
・要約選択は、本文全体の流れ（きっかけ→行動→結果・心の変化）をつかんでから空所に入る語句を考えると選びやすい。
・本文中の関係代名詞（who／that／which）が修飾している語（先行詞）を確認しながら読むと、内容理解が速くなる。
