Gandhi moved to South Africa to work as a lawyer in 1893. It was under British rule at that time, and there was a lot of discrimination. For example, Indians could not go out at night freely or walk on the sidewalk. There were also hotels that did not accept Indian guests.

In 1906, the British made a law that was even more unfair to Indian people. Indians in South Africa got angry and stood up against the law. Gandhi decided to lead a movement to protect their rights. His message was, "Don't follow the law, but don't use violence, even if you are arrested." Soon the jails became full of Indians, and Gandhi himself was sent there. Finally, in 1914, after many years and much effort, the law was removed. It showed that non-violent movements can be effective.

Gandhi returned to India in 1915. In those days, there was a law that the British made for salt. According to the law, only the British could produce or sell salt. Gandhi thought it was unfair.

In 1930, Gandhi decided to walk to the sea and make salt himself. He started with 78 followers. Thousands of people joined him on the way. This non-violent march was called the Salt March. It showed people a new way to fight against discrimination.

Gandhi's peaceful fight continued after that. In 1947, India won independence. Non-violent protest is the legacy that Gandhi left. It has influenced famous leaders, such as Martin Luther King, Jr. and Nelson Mandela.

大問3　適語選択（1点×10）

（1）ア who　先行詞が人（a man）で関係代名詞が主語の働きをする主格の関係代名詞
（2）イ which　先行詞がもの（a picture）で、I found の目的語にあたる目的格の関係代名詞
（3）イ which　先行詞が動物（the dog）の場合、who ではなく which（または that）を使う
（4）ウ were　仮定法のbe動詞は主語に関係なく were を使う
（5）イ had　I wish のあとは動詞の過去形（仮定法）
（6）イ had　If のあとの一般動詞は過去形（仮定法）
（7）イ that　先行詞がもの（Many things）で目的格。教科書本文と同じ形
（8）イ which　先行詞がもの（The backpacks）で主格の関係代名詞
（9）ア beyond　beyond our borders「国境をこえて」（教科書Unit6本文の表現）
（10）ア depends　depend on 〜「〜に頼る」。主語 Japan は3人称単数

大問7　並べかえ・★印（2点×5・完答）

番号記号順★の答え不要語完成文
（1）カ イ ウ エ アイオ（written）The letter she wrote was very moving.
（2）エ イ オ アオウ（violent）Gandhi was a leader who never used violence.
（3）オ ウ ア エオイ（was）If I were in your position, I would apologize to him.
（4）エ オ ア ウオイ（help）If we helped each other more, the world would be a better place.
（5）エ ア ウ イ カウオ（making）This is a product that was made by people from other countries.
（1）「彼女が書いた手紙」は接触節（関係代名詞that/whichの省略）で表せる。The letter のあとに she wrote を続け、was very moving で文を結ぶ。written は意味が重なるため不要語。★は2番目なので wrote ＝ イ。
（2）who never used violence が a leader を後ろから修飾する主格の関係代名詞節。violent（形容詞）は使わないので不要語。★は3番目なので used ＝ オ。
（3）If I were 〜「もし私が〜なら」は仮定法。be動詞は主語に関係なく were を使うので was は不要語。★は1番目なので were ＝ オ。
（4）If we helped 〜「もし私たちが〜すれば」は仮定法。一般動詞 help は過去形 helped にするので、原形の help は不要語。★は2番目なので each ＝ オ。
（5）that was made by people が a product を後ろから修飾する目的格ではなく主格＋受け身の関係代名詞節。making は意味が合わないので不要語。★は3番目なので made ＝ ウ。
