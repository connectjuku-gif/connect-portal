5ハルト（Haruto）がチョコレートの選び方について考えたときの話を読み、あとの問いに答えなさい。［知識・技能（2点×3）］［思考・判断・表現（3点×3）］

When Haruto was shopping with his mother, he noticed a small green and black mark on a chocolate bar. It was the Fairtrade mark, which shows that the cacao used in the product was bought at a fair price from farmers. Haruto's mother explained that cacao, which is grown mainly in West African countries such as Ghana and Ivory Coast, is often sold very cheaply. Because of this, many farming families who grow cacao cannot earn enough money, and some of their children have to work instead of going to school.

Haruto was shocked to learn this. He had always thought that chocolate was just a sweet treat that made people happy, but he had never thought about the people who grow the cacao. Since that day, Haruto has been checking the marks on products before he buys them.

He told his classmates, "A small choice at a store can support someone's life far away. That is something I never knew before."

6ミナミ（Minami）が海洋プラスチックについてのドキュメンタリーを見たときの話を読み、あとの問いに答えなさい。［知識・技能（2点×3）］［思考・判断・表現（3点×3）］

One part of the video introduced a group of young engineers who had designed a system that uses the natural flow of the ocean to collect floating plastic. The system, which does not use any fuel, can gather large amounts of trash without harming fish or other sea animals. Minami was impressed that people close to her own age had created something that could help solve such a large problem.

After watching the documentary, Minami started a small project at school: she and her friends now collect data about the plastic found near their town's river every month, and they share the results with local volunteers who are working to protect the river.

1　語形変化・一語補充（2点×10＝20点）

(1) tastes　(2) gave　(3) called　(4) is visited　(5) were written　(6) cleaned　(7) Have　(8) has　(9) how　(10) for
(1)主語This cakeは3人称単数、現在の状態を表すのでtastes。(2)give型のSVOO（gave+人+物）。(3)call型のSVOC（called+O+C）。(4)(5)受け身はbe+過去分詞、主語に合わせてis/wereを選ぶ。(6)have just+過去分詞（現在完了・完了用法）。(7)現在完了の疑問文はHave/Has+主語+過去分詞。(8)主語Sheなのでhas been+〜ing（現在完了進行形）。(9)「どうやって使うか」なのでhow to。(10)It is 〜 for 人 to 〜の形。

2　並べかえ（不要語1語）（3点×5＝15点）

番号完成文不要語
(1)Tom is wearing the shirt which his mother bought for him.that
(2)The color which I like the best is red.who
(3)This is the piano I play every day.that
(4)Sendai is the city Tom wants to visit.which
(5)Is there anything I can do for you?that
採点：完全一致3点（不要語を正しく除けていること）。(1)は目的格の関係代名詞which（本文中にthatは不要）。(2)は目的格の関係代名詞which（whoは先行詞the colorに使えないため不要）。(3)は接触節（the piano＋I play every day、関係代名詞は入れない）。(4)は接触節（the city＋Tom wants to visit）。(5)も接触節（anything＋I can do for you）。

3　関係代名詞を使った2文結合（3点×5＝15点）

(1) She is a famous dancer that[who] a lot of Japanese people know.
(2) The bag that[which] I got last week was expensive.
(3) Who are the students that[who] Ms. Miller is talking with?
(4) The cookies that[which] Lisa made were very good.
(5) Ann is reading an email that[which] she received yesterday.
いずれも目的格の関係代名詞（that/whichまたはwho）を使って2文をつなぐ。先行詞がもの（bag, cookies, email）の場合はthat/which、人（a famous dancer）の場合はthat/whoが使える。語順・時制が正しく、関係代名詞が入っていれば正解。

4　省略の可否・接触節への書きかえ（2点×5＝10点）

(1) ×　(2) ○　(3) ×
(4) She is a tennis player Sam likes very much.
(5) Do you know the man Miki spoke to at the party?
(1)(3)は主格の関係代名詞（あとに動詞が直接続く）なので省略できない。(2)は目的格の関係代名詞（あとに主語＋動詞が続く）なので省略できる。(4)(5)は目的格の関係代名詞を使わず、先行詞のあとに直接〈主語＋動詞〉を続ける接触節の形にする。
