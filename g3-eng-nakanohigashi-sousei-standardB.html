5レン（Ren）が大学の科学プログラムに参加したときの話を読み、あとの問いに答えなさい。［知識・技能（2点×3）］［思考・判断・表現（3点×3）］

In summer, Ren took part in a one-day science program at a university near his town. In the program, a researcher explained that plastic in the ocean slowly breaks into tiny pieces called microplastics, which are often smaller than five millimeters. These microplastics have been found not only in the sea but also in fish that people eat.

The researcher said, "It is still not completely clear how microplastics affect human health, but many scientists are worried about it." Ren had never imagined that plastic trash thrown away on land could end up affecting food on his own dinner table.

After the program, Ren has been trying to buy products with less plastic packaging, and he has asked his family to join him.

（注）microplastic マイクロプラスチック　millimeter ミリメートル　packaging 包装

②　マイクロプラスチックが人の健康に与える影響は、すでに完全に明らかになっている。（　　）

(3) 本文の内容に合うように、（　）に適する日本語を書きなさい。【知識・技能】

6エミ（Emi）がチョコレート会社とカカオ農家の取り組みを調べたときの話を読み、あとの問いに答えなさい。［知識・技能（2点×3）］［思考・判断・表現（3点×3）］

For her presentation, Emi decided to introduce three chocolate brands which she thinks are working hard for a fairer cacao industry, along with a bar of fair trade chocolate for her classmates to try.

1　語形変化・一語補充（2点×10＝20点）

(1) sounds　(2) gave　(3) called　(4) is visited　(5) were written　(6) finished　(7) Have　(8) has　(9) how　(10) for
(1)sound+形容詞（SVC）。(2)give型のSVOO。(3)call型のSVOC。(4)(5)受け身はbe+過去分詞。(6)have just+過去分詞。(7)現在完了の疑問文。(8)現在完了進行形（has been+〜ing）。(9)how to 〜。(10)It is 〜 for 人 to 〜。

2　並べかえ（不要語1語）（3点×5＝15点）

番号完成文不要語
(1)Here is a letter that was written by Emi.who
(2)He is a pastry chef that can make delicious sweets.which
(3)This is a new store that opened last Sunday.who
(4)The student who visited our school is from Sydney.which
(5)She is the runner who won the race.which
採点：完全一致3点（不要語を正しく除けていること）。(1)(4)(5)は先行詞が「人／もの」で、あとに動詞が続く主格の関係代名詞（who/that）。whichは先行詞に合わず不要。(2)(3)も主格・目的格の関係代名詞の判断がポイント。

3　2文結合（書きかえ）（3点×5＝15点）

(1) The girl who is talking with Ken is my sister.
(2) The dogs that came near me were hungry.
(3) Ms. Oka is a teacher who plays the guitar well.
(4) The letter which made me sad was written by Tom.
(5) The bike he has looks nice.
(1)〜(4)は関係代名詞（主格）を使って2文を1文にする。(5)は関係代名詞を使わない接触節（the bike＋he has）で表す指示がある点に注意。

4　省略の可否・接触節への書きかえ（2点×5＝10点）

(1) ○　(2) ×　(3) ○
(4) The fireworks we saw yesterday were beautiful.
(5) The question Yumi asked me was easy.
(1)(3)は目的格の関係代名詞（she wrote／Mr. Sato caught）なので省略できる。(2)は主格の関係代名詞（あとに動詞makesが直接続く）なので省略できない。(4)(5)は接触節（先行詞＋主語＋動詞）に書きかえる。
