5フェリペ（Felipe）がスペインの祭りに参加したときの話を読み、あとの問いに答えなさい。［知識・技能（2点×3）］［思考・判断・表現（3点×3）］

Felipe's uncle, who has organized local events in his town for over twenty years, invited Felipe to join this year's tomato festival in Spain. Felipe had heard about the festival many times, but this was the first time he had actually taken part in it.

During the festival, Felipe asked one of the organizers why the event started. "Nobody really knows the true reason," the organizer said, "but records show that it has been held almost every year since the 1940s, except for a few years when it was stopped for safety reasons." Felipe was amazed to learn that the town uses more than one hundred tons of tomatoes for a single event.

After the festival, Felipe realized that a tradition doesn't always need a clear reason to be meaningful. He has been reading about other food festivals around the world, and he hopes to take part in one that is completely different from this one next time.

（注）organize 主催する　ton トン（重さの単位）

6真央（Mao）が歴史の授業で人物を調べたときの話を読み、あとの問いに答えなさい。［知識・技能（2点×2）］［思考・判断・表現（3点×2）］

In history class, Mao's teacher asked each student to research a person who had fought for freedom and explain why that person's story still matters today. Mao couldn't decide who to choose, so she asked her teacher for a suggestion.

Her teacher told her about a young activist in South Africa who had spoken out against an unfair system in the 1970s, even though he knew it was dangerous to do so. He died while still young, but his ideas continued to influence people long after his death.

While researching, Mao found it difficult to fully understand the historical background, but she came to realize why it is so important to keep telling stories like his. In her presentation, Mao plans to explain not only what this person did, but also why ordinary people should never stop speaking up for what is fair.

1　文法4択（2点×10＝20点）

(1) ア　(2) ウ　(3) エ　(4) イ　(5) ウ　(6) ア　(7) ウ　(8) イ　(9) ウ　(10) エ
(1)make+O+原形（causative）。(2)give型のSVOO。(3)主語（building）は自分で設計できないので受け身は was designed。(4)未来の受け身（will be+過去分詞）。project自体は自分で終わらせられないので受け身。(5)(6)(7)現在完了・現在完了進行形。(8)too〜for〜to〜。(9)so〜that…。(10)want+人+to〜。

2　並べかえ（3点×5＝15点、不要語を含む）

番号完成文不要語
(1)always makes me cryto
(2)was designed by a famous architectdesign
(3)have been to Hokkaidogone
(4)have you been waitingare
(5)want me to becomebecoming
採点：完全一致3点。不要語を入れてしまったものや語順ミスは2点以下。(2)はbe+過去分詞の受け身なので、原形designは不要。(3)は「行ったことがある」はhave been to〜であり、have goneは「行ってしまって今いない」という意味になるため不要。(4)は現在完了進行形の疑問文（have+主語+been+〜ing）なので、areは不要。(5)はwant+人+to+原形なので、becomingは不要。

3　関係代名詞（主格・目的格混在・発展）（2点×5＝10点）

(1) who　(2) that　(3) ×　(4) that　(5) that
(1)主格・人。(2)先行詞に最上級（the most exciting）がついているときはthatが好まれる（目的格）。(3)目的格の関係代名詞は省略できる（接触節）。(4)先行詞に「the only」がついているときもthatが好まれる（主格）。(5)先行詞が「人＋動物」のときはthatを使う（主格）。

4　後置修飾（2点×5＝10点、不要語を含む）

(1) ウ　(2) エ　(3) ウ　(4) エ　(5) The novel she wrote last year（不要語：which）
(1)能動（singing）。(2)受け身（sung）。(3)能動・状態（wearing）。(4)受け身（taken）。(5)は関係代名詞を使わない接触節なので、whichは不要。

(1) 100　(2) ① ○　② ×　(3) 20
(4) He has been reading about other food festivals around the world.
(5) イ
(6) 解答例：伝統には必ずしも明確な理由が必要なわけではないと気づき、これとはまったく違う食べ物の祭りに今度は参加してみたいと思っている。
(2)② 本文には「安全上の理由で止められた年が何年かあった」とあるので、「一度もない」は誤り。(5) ア・ウ・エはいずれも本文の具体的な事実と矛盾する（今回が初参加、おじは20年以上主催、トマトは100トン以上）。

6　真央の話・読解（知技2点×2・思判3点×2＝10点）

(3) She plans to explain not only what this person did, but also why ordinary people should never stop speaking up for what is fair.
(4) 解答例：歴史的背景をすべて理解するのは難しかったが、このような物語を語り続けることが、普通の人々が公正でないことに対して声を上げ続けることの大切さを伝えるために重要だと気づいた。
(2)① 本文には「まだ若いうちに亡くなった」とあるので、「年を取ってから」は誤り。

7　条件英作文（知技3点・思判7点＝10点）

模範解答：

I want you to visit a few clubs before you decide. It is a good idea for you to ask the club members some questions. My teacher always tells new students to try at least two activities first. I'm sure it will be fun for you to find a club that you really enjoy!（4文）
【B】
It is important for you to think about how much time you can spend after school. I want you to watch a practice before joining. My coach always tells beginners to ask lots of questions. I hope you will find a club that makes your school life more fun!（4文）
