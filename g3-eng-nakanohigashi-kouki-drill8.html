1「わたし」がスペインのトマト祭りに行ったときの話を読み、あとの問いに答えなさい。［知識・技能（2点×4）］［思考・判断・表現（3点×2）］

I love matsuris, Japanese street festivals. Some of the best matsuris for me are the Bon Odori festival in Fukushima and the Naha Great Tug-of-War in Okinawa. ( ① ) there are other festivals around the world that are also very interesting.

Last year I went to Spain and saw a festival there. Many people think that festivals in Spain have bulls, ( ② ) Spanish people have been enjoying bullfighting festivals for many years. But I did not go to a bullfighting festival that time. It was a tomato festival!

In this festival, people did not eat tomatoes or make delicious dishes with tomatoes. They had a tomato fight! On the day of the festival, my father and I went to the middle of the town. ( ③ ) the water cannons were fired at 11 a.m., everybody started to throw tomatoes at each other. All of my clothes became red, and my father had a tomato on the top of his head.

Water cannons were fired again, ( ④ ) we all stopped throwing tomatoes. Everyone looked at each other and couldn't help laughing. It was a really amazing festival for me. I have been waiting to go back to the festival ever since that day.

（注）Bon Odori festival 盆踊り　Naha Great Tug-of-War 那覇大綱挽　bullfighting 闘牛　water cannon 放水銃

I joined a work experience program last week. I worked at a publishing company with two other students for three days. Mr. Watanabe, one of the staff, helped us.

On the first day, Mr. Watanabe explained how to make books. I learned there were so many steps in making books, and I also found that every color in a book was created from only four colors: blue, red, yellow and black!

( ① ) Mr. Watanabe showed us a book on the second day, we were all surprised ( ② ) it was an English workbook we were using at school every day. He said, "Let's have a planning meeting today. Planning is one of the most important parts of our work. Please think about what we should do to make this more understandable to you."

The last day was also exciting. We tried to lay out a spread we worked out the day before. It was so hard that we were not able to finish it without help from other staff. ( ③ ) many other staff gave us advice, and finally we completed it. The pages we completed were only two, ( ④ ) we learned that working together with others can make a big difference.

大問8型ドリル／配点：1＝14点、2＝14点　合計28点
■ 出題範囲メモ・作問方針 実際の中野東3年定期考査 大問8（接続詞穴埋め＋下線部言い換え）は、文法範囲（今回ならProgram5・6）とは関係なく、３年間の総整理問題集の長文読解ページ（「B 長文こつこつトレーニング」）がほぼそのまま引用される独立した大問。このドリルは、総整理問題集 Lesson24「トマトを○○るの?!」（スペインのトマト祭りの話）とLesson27「出版社に潜入！ミカの職場体験レポート」の本文をもとに、原文の流れを生かしながらbut/so/because/whenの穴埋め×4（長文1つにつき1回ずつ）と下線部言い換え×2を加えて作成した（原文そのままの箇所と、設問のために語句を調整した箇所がある）。

1　トマト祭りの話（Lesson24）（知技2点×4・思判3点×2＝14点）

(1) ① But　② because　③ When　④ so
(2) really enjoyed the festival.
(3) have wanted to go back to the festival ever since that day.
① 「日本の祭りが好き」という話から「世界には他にも面白い祭りがある」への転換なのでBut。② 「スペインの祭りには牛がいると思われている」理由を「闘牛を長年楽しんできたから」と説明しているのでbecause。③ 「11時に放水銃が発射されたとき、みんながトマトを投げ合い始めた」という時を表すのでWhen。④ 「放水銃がまた発射された、だから投げるのをやめた」という結果の関係なのでso。(2)(3) it was a really amazing festival for me／have been waiting to go backは、really enjoyed／have wanted toで言いかえられる（意味はほぼ同じ）。

2　ミカの職場体験の話（Lesson27）（知技2点×4・思判3点×2＝14点）

(1) ① When　② because　③ so　④ but
(2) couldn't finish it without help from other staff.
(3) found out that working together with others can make a big difference.
① 「2日目に渡辺さんが本を見せてくれたとき」という時を表すのでWhen。② 「驚いた」理由を「毎日学校で使っている英語のワークブックだったから」と説明しているのでbecause。③ 「大変だった、だから他のスタッフがアドバイスをくれた」という結果の関係なのでso。④ 「完成したのは2ページだけだったが、もっと大切なことを学んだ」という逆接の関係なのでbut。(2) were not able to finish はcouldn't finishで言いかえられる。(3) we learned thatはwe found out thatで言いかえられる。
■ この予想問題のねらい（生徒への指示） ・接続詞穴埋めは、前後の文の関係（理由・結果・時・逆接）を日本語に訳してから考えると選びやすい。「だから」ならso、「なぜなら」ならbecause、「〜のとき」ならwhen、「しかし」ならbutと対応させる習慣をつける。
・下線部言い換えは、本文の表現を日常的によく使う別の言い方（enjoyed, wanted to, couldn't, found outなど）とセットで覚えておくと対応しやすい。
